Third print, first one that closes. The first two snapped at the pin because
the wall was 1.2 mm and the hinge was carrying the whole load on one edge.

Taking the wall to 1.6 mm and moving the pin 4 mm inboard fixed it. The lid
now seats without a catch, which was the point.
